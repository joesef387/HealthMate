    Medication safety tips

    • Take medicines as prescribed and follow the package directions. Ask a pharmacist if anything is unclear.
    • If you miss a dose, follow the medicine’s instructions or ask a pharmacist. Don’t double a dose unless a healthcare professional tells you to.
    • Talk with your doctor or pharmacist before changing or stopping a medicine, and ask them about new or concerning side effects.
    • Store medicines as the label directs, in their original packaging and out of reach of children and pets.

    These are general tips, not personal medical advice. HealthMate is not monitored for emergencies. For severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, contact your local emergency service.
