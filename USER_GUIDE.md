# HealthMate User Guide

**Open HealthMate:** https://joesef387.github.io/HealthMate/

HealthMate helps you organize medication schedules and keep a record of doses
marked as taken or missed. It does not provide medical advice or recommend
doses.

## Create an account or sign in

1. Open the HealthMate link above.
2. Select **Create account** if you are new, then enter your name, email, and
   password. The password must have at least eight characters, including a
   letter and a number.
3. Otherwise, sign in with your email and password.

Google and Facebook buttons are demonstration features; use email sign-in.

## Add a medication

1. Open **Medications**, then select **Add medication**.
2. Enter the medication name and the dose as directed by your healthcare
   professional.
3. Choose how many times per day and set a different time for each dose.
4. Choose whether to enable reminders, then select **Save medication**.

Use **Edit** to update medication details, times, or the reminder preference.
Selecting **Delete** removes the medication from your active schedule by
deactivating its schedules. The backend retains the medication record and
past dose history.

## Record a dose

On **Home**, find the scheduled dose and select **Taken** or **Missed**. The
backend records the status and the time it was recorded. The dashboard updates
the daily summary and adherence calendar.

If you miss a dose, follow the medication instructions or ask a pharmacist.
Do not double a dose unless a healthcare professional tells you to.

## Review or print history

1. Open **History** to review recent dose records.
2. Filter the list by status or medication.
3. To print a report, select the **From** and **To** dates, then choose
   **Print report**. The report summarizes recorded taken and missed doses and
   includes their recorded date and time.

Dates and times are displayed in your account's timezone.

## Reminders

Reminders are best-effort while HealthMate is open in a browser tab. Browser or
device settings may delay notifications, and reminders will not run after the
app is closed.

## Using HealthMate on a phone

Open the public HealthMate link in your phone's browser and sign in with your
account. An internet connection is required to sync your data.

For your privacy, sign out when using a shared device. HealthMate is not
monitored for emergencies. For severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, contact
your local emergency service.
